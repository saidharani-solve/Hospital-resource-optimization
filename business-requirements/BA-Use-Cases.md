Business Analyst Use Cases and User Stories

1. Business Use Case

Use Case: Hospital Resource Monitoring

Business Problem: Hospital management needs better visibility into bed occupancy, patient waiting times, staff utilization, and treatment costs.

Proposed Solution: Use data analysis and dashboards to monitor operational and financial KPIs and identify areas that may need improvement.

Primary User: Hospital Operations Manager

Goal: Support data-driven decisions about hospital resources.

Use Case Flow

1. The analyst collects the available hospital reference dataset.
2. The analyst cleans and validates the data.
3. The analyst calculates operational and financial KPIs.
4. The analyst creates Power BI and Python dashboards.
5. Hospital management reviews the visualizations.
6. Management identifies potential resource-allocation improvements.

 2. User Stories

 User Story 1 — Bed Occupancy

As a hospital operations manager, I want to monitor bed occupancy so that I can identify periods of high resource demand.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Display occupied and available bed information where the dataset supports it.
- Show the occupancy rate clearly.
- Allow users to compare occupancy across relevant departments or periods.

User Story 2 — Patient Waiting Time

As a hospital operations manager, I want to analyze patient waiting times so that I can identify potential delays in patient flow.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Calculate average waiting time from the available data.
- Compare waiting times across departments.
- Highlight departments or periods with higher waiting times.

User Story 3 — Financial Monitoring

As a finance manager, I want to monitor treatment costs and insurance claims so that I can better understand hospital financial patterns.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Display relevant treatment-cost metrics.
- Summarize insurance claim information available in the dataset.
- Present financial indicators through clear charts.

3. Business Analyst Responsibilities

- Understand the business problem and stakeholder needs.
- Document business and functional requirements.
- Convert requirements into user stories and acceptance criteria.
- Coordinate data analysis and dashboard development.
- Validate dashboard outputs against the available dataset.
- Communicate findings and limitations to stakeholders.

4. Expected Outcome

The use cases demonstrate how a Business Analyst can translate hospital operational and financial problems into
measurable requirements and dashboard-based insights.
