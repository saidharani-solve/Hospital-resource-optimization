Business Requirements Document (BRD)

Project Title
Hospital Resource Allocation and Financial Optimization — Apollo Hospitals

1. Business Problem
Hospitals need to manage beds, staff, patient waiting times, and financial resources efficiently. Poor resource allocation can contribute to delays, underutilized resources, and financial inefficiencies.

2. Business Objective
To analyze hospital operational and financial data and identify opportunities to improve resource utilization, patient flow, and financial monitoring.

3. Stakeholders
- Hospital management
- Department heads
- Doctors and nursing teams
- Finance department
- Operations team
- Data analysts and business analysts

4. Business Requirements
- BR-01: Analyze bed occupancy and availability.
- BR-02: Examine patient waiting times and patient flow.
- BR-03: Analyze staff utilization and resource allocation.
- BR-04: Monitor treatment costs and financial indicators.
- BR-05: Analyze claims approval and rejection patterns.
- BR-06: Present key performance indicators through dashboards.

5. Functional Requirements
- Clean and analyze the available dataset.
- Calculate relevant operational and financial KPIs.
- Display results through Power BI and Python dashboards.
- Provide visual summaries to support management decisions.

6. Non-Functional Requirements
- Dashboard information should be clear and easy to understand.
- Data processing should be consistent and reproducible.
- Access to sensitive patient data should be restricted.
- Visualizations should support timely decision-making.

7. Key Performance Indicators
- Bed occupancy rate
- Average patient waiting time
- Staff utilization rate
- Average treatment cost
- Claims approval rate
- Claims rejection rate

8. Expected Business Benefits
- Better visibility into resource utilization.
- Improved monitoring of patient flow.
- More informed financial planning.
- Faster identification of operational bottlenecks.
- Data-driven decision-making.

9. Assumptions and Limitations
This is an independent academic case study using secondary/reference datasets. The analysis is not based on Apollo Hospitals' internal operational systems or live patient data. Findings are illustrative and require validation before real-world implementation.

10. Conclusion
The proposed analytics approach demonstrates how dashboards and data analysis can support hospital operational monitoring and financial decision-making.
