 Key Findings and Business Recommendations

 1. Project Overview

This project explores hospital resource allocation and financial performance using secondary/reference hospital data, Python analysis, and Power BI dashboards.

 2. Key Areas Analyzed

- Bed occupancy and hospital resource utilization
- Patient waiting time and patient flow
- Staff utilization indicators
- Treatment costs and financial patterns
- Insurance claim information

3. Business Recommendations

- Resource Planning: Monitor bed occupancy to identify periods of high demand.
- Patient Flow: Review departments with longer waiting times to identify potential bottlenecks.
- Staff Allocation: Compare staff utilization indicators to support resource planning.
- Financial Monitoring: Track treatment costs and related financial indicators to identify unusual patterns.
- Insurance Claims: Monitor available claim data to understand approval, rejection, or claim-value patterns where the data supports them.
- Dashboard Reporting: Use clear KPI dashboards to help decision-makers monitor operational and financial performance.

4. Limitations

These recommendations are potential areas for investigation, not confirmed improvements. Actual findings should be supported by 
the project's analysis and dashboard results.
